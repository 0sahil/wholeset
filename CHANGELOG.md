# Changelog

User-facing changes in each App Store release. The same notes appear on the [GitHub releases](https://github.com/0sahil/wholeset/releases) page.

## [Unreleased]

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- Reps no longer jump to 100 when you tap reps right after changing the weight.
- The scroll bar no longer appears while you change weight or reps with the Digital Crown.
- The Now Playing page's top bar no longer crowds the time.

### Changed
- For an exercise with no reps yet, the Crown now starts from 10.

### Added
- iPhone: Settings > Report a Problem starts an email to the developer with your app version and device filled in.

## [1.0] - 2026-11-19

First release: a workout tracker for Apple Watch with an iPhone companion.
