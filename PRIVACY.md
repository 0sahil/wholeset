# Wholeset privacy policy

Wholeset is a workout tracker for Apple Watch and iPhone. It has no accounts and no servers, and the developer receives none of your data.

## What Wholeset stores, and where

- **On your devices.** Your routines, weekly plan, exercises, settings and workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes, duration, and the workout's average and maximum heart rate and active energy) are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
- **In your iCloud account.** If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is turned on for Wholeset, the same data syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud database (CloudKit). It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer cannot see or access it.
- **In Apple Health.** With your permission, the Apple Watch app saves each workout to Apple Health as a strength training workout, with its heart rate and active energy, so it appears in Fitness and counts toward your Activity rings. Sets, reps and weights are not written to Apple Health. Workouts you import from other apps are not written to Apple Health either.

## What Wholeset reads

- **Heart rate and active energy** from Apple Health, during a workout on Apple Watch, to show them live and in the workout summary. You can allow or deny this in the Health permissions; logging sets works either way.
- **CSV files you choose.** When you import workouts exported from another app, Wholeset reads only the file you pick or open in it, on your iPhone. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

## What Wholeset does not do

- No tracking, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no third-party code.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Wholeset makes no network connections of its own; the only data that leaves your devices is what Apple's iCloud sync and Apple Health handle for you.

## Deleting your data

- **On a device:** delete the Wholeset app.
- **In iCloud:** on iPhone, open Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage (or Storage), choose Wholeset and delete its data. Do this after deleting the app from all your devices, or the data syncs back.
- **In Apple Health:** open the Health app, find the workouts saved by Wholeset (Browse > Activity > Workouts > Show All Data, or Sharing > Apps > Wholeset) and delete them. Removing Wholeset's access in Health stops new data from being saved.
